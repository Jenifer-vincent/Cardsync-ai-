## Overview

The Business Card Scanner & Contact Automation System is an intelligent contact management solution that digitizes business cards using OCR technology and transforms them into structured digital records.

The platform supports offline card scanning, voice note attachments, and event-based contact organization, enabling professionals to manage and access networking contacts efficiently without relying solely on manual data entry.

The solution helps streamline networking, improve contact management, and enhance accessibility through smart automation features.

## Features

- Business card image scanning and upload
- OCR-based contact information extraction
- Automatic contact validation and storage
- Offline contact access and management
- Voice notes attached to individual contacts
- Event-based contact tagging and categorization
- Search and filter contacts efficiently
- Structured contact database management
- Reduced manual data entry through automation

- ## Problem Statement

Managing business cards manually often leads to misplaced contacts, data entry errors, and difficulty remembering networking interactions.

This project solves these challenges by automatically extracting contact details, organizing contacts based on networking events, enabling offline access, and allowing users to attach voice notes for contextual information and follow-up actions.

## Workflow

1. Scan or Upload Business Card
2. OCR Text Extraction
3. Contact Information Parsing
4. Data Validation
5. Event Tag Assignment
6. Voice Note Attachment (Optional)
7. Database Storage
8. Offline Contact Access & Search
